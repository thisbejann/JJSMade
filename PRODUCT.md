# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Single operator: the builder. A personal shopper/reseller who sources items (shoes, clothes, accessories) from China and resells to customers in the Philippines. Uses the app on-demand to create and track orders through a pipeline (purchase → QC → delivery), monitor profit margins, manage sellers, and review analytics. The audience is exactly one person, so the tool can be opinionated and personal.

## Product Purpose

A personal command center for tracking and visualizing orders and profit. Success means being able to create an order, follow it through the pipeline, and understand where the money is — without losing track of anything. Not a team tool, not a SaaS product: a sharp, personal instrument built for daily use by one operator.

## Brand Personality

Dense, delightful, sharp. Feels like it was designed by someone with taste, for someone with taste. Reference energy: Linear / Raycast — dark, information-dense, with moments of craft that make daily use enjoyable.

Three words: Focused. Refined. Alive.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards, glassmorphism, neon glow effects, purple-to-blue gradients, hero metric grids where all cards look identical.

## Design Principles

1. **Important things demand attention** — profit, pipeline blockages, pending QC items should stand out immediately. Use size, weight, and color hierarchy. Not everything can shout.

2. **Motion as feedback** — animate meaningful moments (approving a QC item, a status changing, data loading in). Keep decorative animation at zero. Every animation should convey state.

3. **Density with breathing room** — pack information in, but use deliberate whitespace to group related things and give the eye places to rest. Tight rows, generous section gaps.

4. **Craft in the details** — this is a personal tool used daily. Small moments of polish (hover states, smooth transitions, sharp typography) make the difference between functional and delightful.

5. **Typography does the work** — rely on font weight and size variation to create hierarchy before reaching for color or icons. The reading order should be obvious at a glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Single-user personal tool. No formal WCAG requirements. Keep it functional and legible for the operator.
